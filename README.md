A free "Insider Track" kit from Ethical AI: tell an AI what you want found, and the kit walks you through the hunt.

## How to use the kit
1. Unzip `sugar-scoop-kit.zip`; read `README.md` and `WHAT_TO_UPLOAD.md` (no passwords or other people's private files).
2. Open any AI chat, upload what's listed, paste `PROMPT.txt`.
3. Follow `HOW_TO_RUN.md`; log results in `TRACKER.csv`.
